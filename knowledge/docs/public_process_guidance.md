# Public injection-molding process guidance

Source: ENGEL, “Optimum holding pressure time in injection moulding” — https://www.engelglobal.com/en/digital-solutions/injection-mould-sampling/holding-pressure-injection-moulding

Holding-pressure time is a quality-driving injection-molding parameter and is normally established after mold setup. The sealing/gate-freeze point should be validated from actual molded parts rather than changed blindly. MoldGuard therefore treats holding-time recommendations as decision support and requires engineer validation before use.

Source: ISO 294-1:2017 overview/reference — https://www.iso.org/standard/66254.html

Injection-molding operating conditions such as melt temperature, mold temperature, injection conditions and holding conditions should be controlled and recorded under steady-state operation. Mold temperature should be assessed after thermal equilibrium. MoldGuard uses measured mold temperature (`Tmold`) rather than inventing a back-pressure feature that is absent from the real training data.

Source: ARBURG technical material on process control — https://www.arburg.com/

Stable injection speed/pressure control and correct switchover into holding pressure are central to repeatable part quality. Recommendations should remain within a validated operating window and should be reviewed when the planned setting is outside the distribution represented by historical production data.
