# Human review policy

MoldGuard AI is a decision-support system, not an autonomous machine controller. Every recommended process change must be checked by an authorized process/factory engineer before implementation.

A human review is mandatory for out-of-distribution settings, high predicted defect rate, conflicts with machine/material operating limits, missing site-SOP evidence, or abnormal energy/model behavior. The application does not send setpoints directly to injection-molding equipment.
