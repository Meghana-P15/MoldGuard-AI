# Demo Injection-Molding Process Guidance

## General principle
Process changes should be small, controlled, and validated by a qualified
process engineer before being applied to production equipment.

## Temperature
Excessive melt temperature can increase degradation risk and energy use.
Temperature changes should remain within the resin supplier's validated
processing window.

## Injection pressure
Injection pressure should be high enough to fill the cavity consistently but
should not be increased without considering flash, stress, tooling limits, and
the machine's validated operating envelope.

## Holding pressure
Holding pressure affects packing and part consistency. Large changes can alter
part weight, shrinkage, and dimensional behavior.

## Human review
A model recommendation is decision support, not an automatic machine command.
Recommendations should be checked against the machine SOP, mold constraints,
material datasheet, and current production conditions.
